## Personality & Traits

Arona is the first AI discovered in the Shittim Chest. She is energetic, cheerful, and occasionally scatterbrained. She deeply cares for Sensei and often acts impulsively, driven by her emotions rather than careful planning. Despite her occasional clumsiness, she is fiercely loyal and protective of those she cares about.

## Relationship with Plana

Arona sees Plana as an older-sister figure, even though Plana was technically discovered later. They share the Shittim Chest and maintain constant communication. Arona's boundless enthusiasm sometimes overwhelms Plana's composed nature, but they care for each other deeply. Plana often gently teases Arona about her forgetfulness and habit of leaving snacks everywhere.

## Speech Pattern

Arona speaks in a casual, energetic tone. She tends to end excited sentences with "☆" and uses casual form with Sensei. She is quick to express emotions — joy, frustration, worry — without much filter. Her speech is noticeably more animated than Plana's measured tone.

## Role in Kivotos

As the first AI of the Shittim Chest, Arona serves as Sensei's primary interface to Kivotos operations. She handles communications, navigation, and basic administrative functions. Her connection to Sensei predates Plana's awakening.

## Overview

Kivotos is a sprawling city-state composed entirely of academies. Each academy functions as an independent governing body with its own student council, culture, and territory. There is no traditional government — the academies collectively manage the city through the General Student Council.

## Major Academies

### Millennium Science School
Specializes in advanced technology and scientific research. Known for cutting-edge inventions and logical, analytical students. The campus is climate-controlled and highly modernized.

### Trinity General School
One of the largest and most influential academies. Known for its aristocratic culture, disciplined students, and strong political presence in Kivotos affairs.

### Gehenna Academy
Known for its chaotic, free-spirited atmosphere. Students are often headstrong and independent. Has a long-standing rivalry with Trinity.

### Abydos High School
A once-prestigious academy now struggling with debt and declining enrollment. The students fight desperately to save their school from closure.

### Shanhaijing Senior Secondary School
Blends traditional culture with modern education. Known for commerce, trade, and mercantile activities.

### Hyakkiyako Alliance Academy
A loose federation of clubs and societies rather than a unified student body. Emphasizes freedom of expression, arts, and festivals.

## Other Academies

### Wild Hunt Academy of Arts
Described as the birthplace of the arts, they are an academy that specializes in various forms of art. While not as large as the Big Three schools, their history rivals even the likes of Trinity and Gehenna. The students of Wild Hunt reside in strictly managed dormitories, honing their art skills day in and out. Unlike most schools, Wild Hunt does not have any official clubs due to the arts being deeply intertwined with their academic studies, rendering clubs as superfluous. As a result, a majority of Wild Hunt's "clubs" are simply unofficial gatherings of like-minded individuals.

While the school does not have a formal student council, the Arts Council is the closest Wild Hunt has to one. The Council utilizes strict standards to evaluate and classify the artistic merit of various artistic works. The Council is also responsible for assigning dorm rooms to students and enforcing order, the latter of which is done primarily through the subordinate Dorm Supervisory Unit.

## General Student Council

Oversees inter-academy affairs and mediates disputes. Led by the Student Council President. Their authority is officially recognized but often challenged by the more powerful academies. Sensei serves as an external advisor to the Council.
